YOUR DETECTIVE TOOLKIT

Three things to spot

▰
A face is a flat surface.

Imagine laying your palm flat on it.

╱
An edge is a line where two faces meet.

Trace it with your finger.

●
A vertex is a corner.

Tap the point where edges meet. More than one? We call them vertices.
